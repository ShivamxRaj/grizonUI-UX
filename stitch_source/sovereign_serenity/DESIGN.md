## Brand & Style

The design system embodies sovereign Indian technological craftsmanship—an intentional fusion of mathematical rigor, institutional trust, and warm, illuminated tranquility. Built to communicate frontier-scale capability without the frantic neon clichés of Western Silicon Valley AI, the visual language draws inspiration from cultural resonance (saffron dawn, ethereal periwinkle twilight) and classical scholarly clarity.

The aesthetic blends **Modern Institutional Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Luminescence**:
- **Pristine Atmospheric Canvas:** Clean, expansive white space grounded by delicate, ethereal radial glows of sacred saffron, morning amber, and serene periwinkle lilac hovering like ambient morning light.
- **Academic Serenity:** Literary, humanist display typography anchored by razor-sharp geometric sans-serif interfaces and monospaced diagnostic labels.
- **Glass & Hairline Precision:** Tactile yet weightless containers featuring semi-translucent glass backdrops, subtle 1px border dividers, and delicate diffused shadows that feel physically calibrated and mathematically exact.
- **Dignity & Restraint:** High contrast deep slate ink against soft surfaces, eliminating chaotic accents in favor of purposeful, quiet confidence suitable for national infrastructure and enterprise leaders.

## Colors

The color palette reflects dawn light breaking across an architectural stone temple—warmth radiating from above while deep obsidian anchors the earth.

- **Primary (`#1E2033` / `obsidian-ink` `#0C0D14`):** Deep astronomical slate and obsidian used for primary text, solid primary interactive states, and key brand marks. Delivers uncompromised contrast and solemn authority.
- **Secondary (`#F47D31` / `saffron-aura` `#FF9933`):** Radiant saffron-amber representing Indian sovereign vitality, invention, and clarity. Used sparingly for illuminated hero radial washes, key achievement metrics, and subtle active indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#7B86E8` / `lilac-glow` `#A5BBFC`):** Ethereal lavender-indigo capturing digital cognition, linguistic breadth, and calm intelligence. Powers radial ambient backdrops and secondary model badges.
- **Neutral (`#696E88` / `slate-surface` `#F4F5F9`):** Balanced, low-chroma slate neutrals for body text, meta annotations, structural container fills, and delicate hairline separators.

### Ambient Gradients & Atmospheric Light
The canvas relies on radial background halos at the top viewport and behind key interactive components:
- `hero-saffron-conic`: Soft gradient blur fading from `#FF9933` (opacity: 18%) through `#FFF5EB` to transparent `#FCFCFD`.
- `hero-lilac-ambient`: Diffuse radial glow of `#A5BBFC` (opacity: 22%) bleeding across the lower-third hero perimeter.
- Never employ saturated flat color fills for large layout sections; preserve luminance and air.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is calibrated to evoke the prestige of national publishing alongside technical software instrumentation:

1. **Serif Display Voice (`Newsreader`):** Applied to top-level section banners, hero declarations, and manifesto headlines. Its literary, high-contrast strokes imbue artificial intelligence with humanistic stewardship, philosophical depth, and historic permanence. It is deliberately set with tight, elegant negative tracking.
2. **Body & Interaction Engine (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, open-aperture geometric sans that balances high legibility across Indian language scripts with modern enterprise clarity. Used for instructional narratives, card headers, tabs, and form components.
3. **Telemetry & Technical Monospace (`JetBrains Mono`):** Reserved for technical metadata, sovereign status chips, telemetry metrics (SLA, latency, token count), and code execution payloads. It lends rigorous mathematical verification to the user experience.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on mathematical balance, quiet breathing room, and structured alignment:

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column responsive fluid grid with maximum container width constrained to `1240px` for optimal reading flow, expanding to `1400px` for complex interactive workbench canvases.
- **Rhythmic White Space:** Sections are segregated by generous vertical margins (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`), allowing the eye to reset between complex conceptual domains.
- **Breakpoint Philosophy:**
  - **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column stack, outer canvas margin `1rem`, compact gutter `1rem`. Floating actions dock securely to the viewport bottom.
  - **Tablet (641px - 1024px):** 6-column fluid structure, outer margin `2.5rem`, cards flow into 2-column matrices.
  - **Desktop (> 1025px):** 12-column structured layout with wide `4rem` to `6rem` margins, enabling 3-column architecture matrices and side-by-side terminal/preview demonstrations.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy eschews heavy physical drop shadows in favor of **luminosity, frosted glass refraction, and hairline definition**:

- **Subsurface Glows:** Ambient atmospheric depth is achieved via soft background radial gradients with 60px–120px blur radii, providing spatial context beneath interactive surfaces.
- **Frosted Glassmorphism (Tier 1 Surface):** Interactive consoles, floating navigations, and elevated cards employ `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` paired with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` background fills and a crisp `1px solid rgba(229, 231, 235, 0.6)` perimeter edge.
- **Feathered Ambient Shadows (Tier 2 Elevation):** Popovers, active prompt consoles, and dropdowns utilize ultra-diffused, dual-layer shadows tinted with deep slate:
  - `0 1px 2px rgba(12, 13, 20, 0.04), 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(12, 13, 20, 0.06)`
- **Hairline Framing:** Component borders never exceed 1px. In light theme, borders rest at 8%–14% opacity slate, ensuring containers establish structural clarity without visual heaviness.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture balances contemporary software accessibility with calm mathematical precision:

- **Level 2 (Rounded):** Standard elements (buttons, input fields, badges, code snippets) feature `0.5rem` (`8px`) corner radius for approachable utility.
- **Containers & Glass Panels (`rounded-xl`):** Feature `1rem` to `1.25rem` (`16px`–`20px`) curvature, softening large visual masses like the central interactive workbench and feature cards.
- **Pill Elements (`rounded-full`):** Category chips, model status badges, and pill buttons (such as primary CTAs) leverage complete border-radius to symbolize cohesion and sovereign completeness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Sovereign CTA:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), deep obsidian background (`#1E2033`), crisp pure white text (`#FFFFFF`), subtle top-edge highlight inset shadow (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`). Hover triggers a gentle upward translation (`-1px`) and an ethereal glow.
- **Secondary Ghost / Glass Button:** Pill-shaped, semi-transparent white backdrop (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`), hairline border (`#E5E7EB`), text in `#1E2033`. Hover: background fills to `#F4F5F9`.
- **Text Action Links:** Understated typography with trailing micro-arrow (`→`) that shifts 3px right on hover; zero background clutter.

### Interactive Workbench Console
- **Unified Query Sandbox:** Multi-tabbed glassmorphic shell containing conversational inputs, model switchers, and workflow actions.
- Features horizontal pill tab navigation with hairline active indicator borders and clean typographic tabs (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, 500 weight).
- Inner prompt container rests on an ultra-light recessed background (`#F9FAFB`) with an inner focus border glow using tertiary periwinkle (`#A5BBFC`).

### Chips & Sovereign Badges
- **Linguistic & Metric Chips:** Height `24px`–`28px`, rounded-full, mono metadata text (`label-caps`).
- **Telemetry Indicators:** Embedded micro-dot status pulses (e.g., green for uptime, saffron for sovereign compute status), framed in `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)` surface fills with 1px border.

### Feature & Model Cards
- **Tonal Bento Grid Cards:** Clean rectangular cards with subtle border borders (`#E5E7EB/60`) and soft white surface gradients.
- Header showcases an illustrative icon glyph encased in an illuminated iridescent gradient square (`48x48px`, saffron-to-lilac subtle transition).
- Subdued meta footer containing live metric outputs (tokens/sec, latency, coverage) rendered in `JetBrains Mono`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Base Input:** Height `44px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5E7EB`, padding `0 1rem`. Focus state removes default outline and transitions border to `#1E2033` with a diffused `0 0 0 3px rgba(165, 187, 252, 0.35)` focus ring.
- **Interactive Search / Prompt Input:** Expansive multi-line container with embedded file-attachment and voice-action buttons docked gracefully in the interior baseline.

### Lists & Navigation
- **Floating Top Bar:** Glassmorphism sticky header (`backdrop-blur-md`, `bg-white/80`), razor-thin bottom border (`border-b border-slate-200/50`).
- Menu links in muted slate `#696E88`, transitioning to `#0C0D14` on active hover with no underlines.
