FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Auto Mode?

expand_more
Auto Mode is the default way to chat on GriZon AI. Each message is evaluated by an ultra-lightweight sovereign classifier and routed to the best eligible specialised agent for your plan — so you do not have to pick manually unless you want to.

How does the credit system work?

expand_more
Each plan includes monthly credits. Agent responses consume credits based on actual token processing and agent multipliers (e.g. Deep Research and Video Analyser involve multi-step reasoning trees). You can view your live credit ledger in your workspace wallet at any moment. Paid tiers feature rollover credits.

Is my data secure on GriZon AI?

expand_more
Yes. All conversational data and uploaded artifacts are isolated with 256-bit encryption in transit and at rest within sovereign cloud compute clusters. Most critically, GriZon strictly adheres to a zero-training charter: your data is never used to train public or foundational models.

Can I switch plans at any time?

expand_more
Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel directly from your sovereign account settings with zero friction. There are no lock-in agreements or cancellation penalties.

What can the Video Analyser do?

expand_more
On Pro and Elite plans, the Video Analyser agent reviews uploaded video files (MP4, MOV, WebM) — breaking down footage frame by frame to describe scenes, extract on-screen OCR text, log event timestamps, transcribe dialogues, and answer deep semantic questions about the footage.
